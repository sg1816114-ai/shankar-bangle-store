Understanding Status & Button Options

Everything you need to know about browsing our visual catalog, checking availability, and connecting directly with Sandeep Gupta.

1. In Stock vs On Order

In Stock

Ready for direct viewing and dispatch at our Firozabad store immediately.

On Order

Crafted on custom demand by our artisans. Sandeep Gupta will update you on crafting timelines.

* The store owner can toggle this status at any time.

2. How the Buttons Work

View Full

Opens a crystal-clear high-definition zoom modal to inspect cut patterns, luster, and stone clarity up close.

Inquire

Launches WhatsApp with the exact Design SKU code pre-filled, so Sandeep Gupta knows instantly which piece you want.

Pure visual showcase. No quantities or volume counts needed.

The Heritage of Shankar Bangle Store

Shankar Bangle Store is based in Firozabad, the City of Bangles. We bring together the charm of traditional designs and the freshness of modern styles, offering collections that match changing trends and customer preferences. Our aim is to provide beautiful designs and reliable wholesale service to shop owners and bangle retailers. We welcome buyers from across India and around the world to explore our collection and connect with us for wholesale enquiries.

Thank you for visiting Shankar Bangle Store! Explore our collection of bangle designs, neatly organised into different categories. Find the designs that best suit your needs and preferences, and contact us directly for wholesale enquiries and orders. We look forward to serving you!
